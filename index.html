Hi!, I'm Luis Martí — an AI scientist, currently Chief Science Officer at Docugami (Seattle, USA) and Science Director at the Inria Chile Research Center, the Chilean center of Inria, the French National Institute for Research in Digital Science and Technology. I am also an Adjunct Professor at the Institute of Astrophysical Studies, Universidad Diego Portales.

My research focuses on System 2 AI and neuro-symbolic ‘agentic’ reasoning, causality, natural language understanding, human-AI cooperation and active learning, conformal prediction and underspecification, multi-modal/multi-task problems, physics-informed ML, nature-inspired metaheuristics and evolutionary multi-objective optimization, and all that.

I apply my research in real-world domains like document understanding, astrophysics/astronomy, climate modeling, ecology, process optimization, energy, mining, and many more. I am interested in applications of AI for science and AI for good, its policies and governance.

Paul Erdős number: 3, Alan M. Turing number: 5.
