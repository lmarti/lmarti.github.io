## Selected research projects

- **MOFOMO** — multimodal foundation models for a digital twin of the ocean, with pilots on harmful algal blooms in southern Chile and coral bleaching in Moorea; Google.org Impact Challenge: AI for Science, 2026–2029 (co-PI).
- **OcéanIA** — AI, machine learning and modeling for understanding the oceans and climate change; Inria Challenge, 2020–2026 (co-PI).
- **HUEMUL** — Human+AI ensembled machine translation for underrepresented languages, 2024–2026 (co-PI).
- **LLACA** — knowledge-graph-augmented LLMs for cultural references, 2025–2027 (co-PI).
- **Stargaze** — ML and explainable AI for understanding astronomical observations, with the European Southern Observatory and the ALeRCE LSST broker, 2020–2025 (co-PI).
- **L.O.V.E.** — LSST Operators Visualization Environment, with the Vera C. Rubin Observatory (AURA/NSF), 2020–2024.
- **GreenAI** and **E-MISTRAL** — towards ecologically viable machine learning and an environmental monitoring autonomous sailboat; CLIMAT/STIC AMSUD regional cooperation, 2019–2023 (PI of E-MISTRAL).
- **Vendimia 5.0** — human-machine planning to optimize grape harvest and winemaking operations, with the Concha y Toro Winery Center of Research and Innovation, 2024–2030 (co-PI).
- **FrostForecast** — IoT and machine learning for frost prediction in cherry fields and vineyards, CORFO, 2019–2022 (co-PI).
